Algorithm Developers @ HRT
Algorithm Developers research, develop, and test 
novel order execution and model training methods 
to increase trading efficiency and maintain 
ongoing profitability.
Areas of expertise:
Programming in Python and/or C++
Statistical analysis, numerical 
programming, or machine learning 
in Python, Pandas/Numpy.
A typical day involves applying rigorous statistical 
analysis to vast quantities of market and financial 
data to produce predictive trading models.

Built with Delta
Show off how you worked together in Delta. Extra points 
for projects that solve real problems.
Work sync (or async) alongside teammates and agents. Use Delta from 
brainstorming/ideation, building, all the way through testing. Share your 
Delta threads to show examples of how you collaborated.

=== PAGE 28 ===
What We’re Looking For
● Projects that feel fast -  snappy UX, zero unnecessary friction.
● Projects that solve a real problem. Not a demo. Something you’d 
actually open tomorrow.
● Projects that are thoughtful. Clean code, smart architecture, good 
decisions.
Prompts to Get You Started
• What slows you down when you’re coding? What if that friction 
disappeared?
• What’s broken about collaborating on code with teammates or AI?
• What would you build if latency wasn’t a constraint?
• What context do you lose every time you switch tasks — and how do 
you get it back?

=== PAGE 29 ===
How You Win
Speed & Performance (30%) — Does it feel fast? Is the UX snappy and 
responsive?
Technical Implementation (25%) — Clean code, smart architecture, good 
use of collaboration features.
Innovation (25%) — Fresh idea. Real problem. New angle.
Polish & Usability (20%) — Would you actually use this? Does it delight?
