Your microphone

Use a quiet room, hold the phone about 15 cm from your mouth (or use a headset mic), and make sure no speakers are playing.

Press the button, then say something.
The browser isn't asking for the microphone / says blocked

Safari: menu Safari → Settings → Websites → Microphone, set 127.0.0.1 to Allow, then reload this page.
Chrome: click the icon left of the address bar → Site settings → Microphone → Allow, then reload.
Mac system: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, and switch your browser on.
